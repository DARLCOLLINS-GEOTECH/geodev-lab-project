# Healthcare Accessibility in Abuja Municipal Area Council (AMAC)

## The Question

**Which operational wards in Abuja Municipal Area Council (AMAC), FCT Abuja, have the greatest number of residents living more than 2 km from the nearest mapped healthcare facility?**

## Why It Matters

Identifying communities with poor geographic access to healthcare can help health planners, government agencies, and development organisations understand where gaps in healthcare coverage exist. The results could support decisions on where new healthcare facilities, outreach services, mobile clinics, or other interventions may be most needed.

## Data Required

* **Healthcare facility locations** – Point locations of mapped healthcare facilities in FCT Abuja.
* **Gridded population estimates** – Population estimates showing where people live across the study area.
* **Operational ward boundaries** – Polygon boundaries of operational wards in FCT Abuja, from which AMAC wards will be selected.

## Data Sources

1. **Healthcare Facility Locations** – GRID3 NGA – Health Facilities v3.0, Center for Integrated Earth System Information Network (CIESIN), Columbia University.
   [GRID3 Nigeria Health Facilities Data](https://data.grid3.org/datasets/827e3638dc204f4b9ddbbd19b00954d6/about)

2. **Gridded Population Estimates** – Nigeria Population v3.0, WorldPop, University of Southampton / GRID3.
   [WorldPop Nigeria Population v3.0 Data](https://data.worldpop.org/repo/wopr/NGA/population/v3.0/)

3. **Operational Ward Boundaries** – GRID3 NGA – Operational Wards v3.0, Center for Integrated Earth System Information Network (CIESIN), Columbia University.
   [GRID3 Nigeria Operational Wards v3.0]([[https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=45cd2ef592094d12aca43113a90a6054&utm_source=chatgpt.com](https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-wards-v3-0/about)

## What I Would Build

I would build an **interactive healthcare accessibility map of AMAC** showing healthcare facility locations, 2 km healthcare accessibility zones, and operational wards classified according to the estimated number of residents living outside those zones. The map would allow users to quickly identify and compare wards with the greatest potential gaps in geographic access to healthcare services.
